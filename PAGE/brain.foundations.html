The Universe Inside Your Brain

Your brain is made of billions of neurons constantly communicating through electrical and chemical signals. Every memory, emotion, movement, dream, and thought exists because of these tiny connections. Brain Foundations is a journey into understanding how the human brain is wired.

Neurons

Neurons are the core communication cells of the nervous system.

What Are Neurons?

Neurons are specialized cells designed to carry information through electrical impulses and chemical signals. The human brain contains around 86 billion neurons.

How Neurons Are Wired

Neurons are connected in massive pathways called neural networks. Each neuron has dendrites, a cell body, and an axon.

Electrical Signals

Neurons communicate using tiny electrical impulses called action potentials. These signals travel rapidly across the nervous system.

Synapses

The Synaptic Gap

Neurons communicate through microscopic spaces called synapses. Neurotransmitters travel across these gaps.

Learning and Memory

Learning happens when synaptic connections strengthen through repetition and experience.

Neural Communication

Neurotransmitters help neurons communicate different kinds of information.

Dopamine & Motivation

What Is Dopamine?

Dopamine is a neurotransmitter involved in reward, motivation, movement, and attention.

The Reward System

The brain has reward circuits that reinforce learning and motivation.

Attention and Focus

Dopamine plays an important role in executive functioning and focus regulation.

Neuroplasticity

Changing Neural Pathways

Neuroplasticity is the brain's ability to reorganize itself by forming new neural connections.

The Brain and Experience

Experiences influence the architecture of the brain.

The Future of Neuroscience

Neuroscience continues exploring consciousness, memory, neurodivergence, and brain technology.
